# Where First Order Outcomes Start Showing Up

Second-order outcomes are not the first things MNKY Math tries to produce.  
  
They are what can begin to happen when system sight, behavioral humility, outcome literacy, agency recovery, and design conscience start showing up in ordinary participation.  
  
They appear in meetings, leadership, management, work design, customer experience, technology adoption, civic judgment, and the way people challenge systems without becoming merely negative.  
  
These outcomes are not guarantees.  
  
They are signs that a different form of participation is beginning to take root.

## Better meetings  
  
People stop arguing only over opinions and start identifying the system assumptions underneath the disagreement.  
  
Instead of stopping at:  
  
- Sales is wrong.  
- Product is slow.  
- Support is complaining.  
- Leadership is disconnected.  
  
They begin asking:  
  
- What system is each group responding to?  
- What signal is each group optimizing around?  
- What constraint is each group absorbing?  
- What outcome is each group trying to protect?  
  
The meeting changes when the disagreement is no longer treated as a personality problem.  
  
It becomes a system-reading problem.
