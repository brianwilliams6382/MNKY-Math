##MM Content Types

Sample text

• essay = polished essay/article or published piece 

• concept = reusable definition/framework node 

• story = narrative entry point or illustrative story, such as musical chairs 

• observation = shorter system noticing 

• reflection = more personal/philosophical thinking 

• field-note = real-world observed system behavior 

• case-study = structured diagnostic/application piece

• definition = a page that lives in directory '_reference' and is primarily established to provide a popover definition of a word or phrase
