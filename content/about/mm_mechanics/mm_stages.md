## Our Content Stages



Seed

A raw phrase, observation, question, or emerging idea.

Seedling

A promising idea with early shape, but not enough structure yet.

Sapling

A structured concept or thesis with definition, examples, and early uses.

Rooted

A stable MNKY idea that has been tested across multiple articles, examples, or models.

Canopy

A major organizing idea that shelters, connects, or branches into many other concepts.
