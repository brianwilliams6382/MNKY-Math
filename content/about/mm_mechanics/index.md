### 1. The "M" Axis (The Maturity Journey)

Using "M" labels creates a systemic cadence. Here is how they map to your logic:

- **Awareness → Mirroring:** (The FM0/New User). At this stage, they are "mirroring" the patterns you've pointed out. They see the system reflected back at them for the first time.
    
- **Application → Manipulating:** (The FM1-3/Engaged User). This isn't "manipulation" in the sinister sense; it is the **Mechanical** act of adjusting the dials and pulling the levers of a system to change an outcome.
    
- **Design → Mastering (or Mending):** (The FM4/Math MNKY). This is the stewardship phase—the person who can repair a broken system or engineer a new one from scratch.
    

### 2. Highlighting the "Math MNKY" (The Troop)

To turn a visitor into a "Math MNKY," the site's UI needs to move from **Passive Consumption** to **Active Invitation**. Here are two "Systemic Design" ideas for your sidebar or footer:

#### A. The "Troop Progress" Sidebar Widget

Since you have the **FM Ladder**, you can place a small, styled box in the sidebar (under the Graph View or Explorer) titled **"The MNKY Lab."**

- **The Text:** _"This observation is rated FM2. Are you seeing a different friction? Join the Troop and help us tune the logic."_
    
- **The Link:** This points to a "Join Us" page or a specific discussion area.
    
- **The Psychological Nudge:** By labeling the _content_ with a level, you subtly challenge the "Math MNKY" persona to validate or "Master" the concept.
    

#### B. The "Mending the Math" Footer

In the footer of every page, instead of a standard "Contact Me," use a **"Stewardship Call."**

> **"Systems are never finished, only tuned."** Found a bug in this theory? Have a case study that breaks this model? **[Become a Math MNKY]** — Contribute to the Troop's Collective Logic.

### 3. The "Math MNKY" Technical Identity

For the **Math MNKY** persona (Persona 3), the site should treat them like a "Peer Reviewer."

In the future, when a Math MNKY contributes a story or a challenge, you can add a custom key to that page: `contributor: [[The Math MNKY Name]]`

On the graph, this creates a node for that person. Over time, the "Troop" becomes a visible part of the neural network—a cluster of people physically connected to the theories they've helped "Mend."

### 4. The Foundation Check

You mentioned not wanting to overbuild. The beauty of the **Mirroring / Manipulating / Mastering** axis is that it only requires **one line in your Frontmatter**:

`stage: Mirroring`

This is low-effort now but provides high-leverage filtering later. You could eventually have a button on the homepage that says "Show me all **Mastering** content" for the users who want the deep-dive tools.

**How do those "M" words—Mirroring, Manipulating, and Mastering—sit with your sense of brand integrity?** Do they feel like they capture the clinical reality of systems work, or do they feel like they're veering too far into "Marketing Speak"?

---
The shift from **Manipulating** to **Modeling** is a high-level "Systems Repair" on the brand logic.

**Modeling** carries a much more constructive energy. In a pharmacy or a corporate environment, a "Model" is a gold standard—it’s something to emulate, test, and refine. It bridges the gap between simply seeing a pattern (**Mirroring**) and eventually owning the architecture (**Mastering**).

### 1. The Triple-M Axis: A Breakdown

|**Stage**|**Action**|**Mindset**|**User Value**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Mirroring**|Observation|"I see the system."|Validation & Awareness|
|**Modeling**|Application|"I am testing the system."|Utility & Personal Agency|
|**Mastering**|Stewardship|"I am tuning the system."|Authority & Collective Growth|

This progression is clean, professional, and—most importantly—it survives the "Internal Wording Test." If a user sees "Modeling" in your metadata, they feel like they are becoming a practitioner, not a subject.
