# Connect with Us

Most publishing asks people to follow the thinking.

MNKY Math invites you to enter the thinking.

If you have a system observation, example, suggestion, challenge, question, or related concept to share, there are two simple ways to connect.

## LinkedIn

The primary public doorway for MNKY Math is LinkedIn.

You can [follow the MNKY Math page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mnky-math), comment on public posts, or send a direct message.

LinkedIn is also the simplest way to follow new essays, site updates, framework notes, and emerging ideas.

## Email

If you want to share something in a more private way, you can email:

**thetroop@mnkymath.com**

This may be a better path for examples, questions, or observations that feel too specific, unfinished, sensitive, or difficult to share publicly.

You do not need to have a fully formed idea.

MNKY Math does not need agreement as much as it needs useful friction, grounded examples, clearer language, and better seeing.
