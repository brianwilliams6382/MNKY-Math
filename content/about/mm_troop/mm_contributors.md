Your outreach goals shift the **"People"** page from a standard "Meet the Team" roster to a **Strategic Recruiting Tool**. You aren't just showing who is there; you're showing the _empty chairs_ you want to fill.

### 1. The Strategy: The "Join the Troop" Pitch

Since your goal is to find collaborators and co-authors, the "People" room shouldn't be a folder of empty bios. It should be a **Manifesto for Participation**.

**The Content Hierarchy for `content/about/people/index.md`:**

- **The Lead:** "MNKY Math is a collaborative laboratory. We believe the best systemic insights come from the intersection of different industries."
    
- **The Call to Action (Goal 2 & 3):** A section titled **"Become a MNKY,"** outlining exactly what you're looking for (directional thoughts, co-authorship).
    
- **The LinkedIn Bridge (Goal 1):** A direct link to your LinkedIn profile with a prompt: _"We build in public. Follow the trail on LinkedIn to join the conversation."_
