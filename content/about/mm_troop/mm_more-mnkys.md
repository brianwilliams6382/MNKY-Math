# The World needs more MNKYs  
  
Modern systems do not need people to be whole.  
  
...  _They need people to be predictable._  
  
These systems do not require agency.  
  
... _They require participation that feels automatic._  
  
That is why MNKY Math keeps returning to a simple line:  
  
> **The World needs more MNKYs.**  
  
![[/static/more_mnkys.webp]]

👉 A **==monkey==** is not a bad person.
  
**_It is a role systems often train people to play_**: reactive, compliant, hurried, optimized, measured, sorted, nudged, and moved without fully seeing what is moving them. Over time, those repeated signals, pressures, defaults, and rewards can train [[def_automaticity|automaticity]] — participation that happens with less awareness, less friction, and more reactive forms of choice.
  
👍 A **==MNKY==** is someone practicing a different kind of participation.
  
Not untouched by systems.  
  
Not outside them.  
  
Not perfectly independent.  
  
But more aware of the systems shaping our behavior, more careful with measurement, more able to recover agency, and more willing to ask what the system is quietly teaching us to become.  
  
Systems may reward monkeys.  
  
### But the World needs MNKYs. And more of them. 
  
➡️ People who can see more clearly.  
  
➡️ Participate more intentionally.  
  
➡️ Build with more conscience.  
  
➡️ And protect agency where systems quietly try to reduce it.
