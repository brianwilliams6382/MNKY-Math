# Stand Somewhere. On Purpose.

You can’t unsee it now. 

The tradeoffs. The shifts.

The way systems move things — quietly, consistently — across people, across time, across you.

Once you see it, something changes.

Not in the system.

> In you.

---
## You Were Never Outside It

It’s easy to think of systems as something external.

Workplaces. Platforms. Policies. Process. Tools. Targets.

Things you participate in. Actions you perform.

Even just being present is participation. There is no non-participation state.

> But participation isn’t neutral.

The moment you’re inside a system, it begins shaping how you move within it.

- What you prioritize.
- What you ignore.
- What you tolerate.
- What you optimize for — without realizing it.

> Participation (_passive or intentional_) means you’re already being shaped. 

Not eventually.

Not hypothetically.

Now.

---

## Drift Feels Like Default

Most of this doesn’t feel like a decision.

It feels like flow. Like floating. Like being pushed more than choosing.

- You follow the incentives.
- You respond to the pressures.
- You adapt to what’s rewarded.

And over time, that adaptation becomes orientation.

Not because you chose it — but because you stayed in it long enough for it to become “the way”.
<br><br><br>
**_Not choosing doesn’t remove the tradeoff._**

**==It assigns it for you.==**

---

## The Question Isn’t the System

It’s tempting to focus outward.

- To analyze the system.
- To critique it.
- To try to understand how it works.

But that only gets you so far.

Because the system will keep doing what it does.

- Optimizing. 
- Shifting. 
- Distributing tradeoffs — whether you agree with them or not.
<br><br>

**_The question isn’t how the system works._**

**==It’s how you choose to engage with it.==**

---
## Standing Is a Decision

There’s no neutral position.

No frictionless path. No place where tradeoffs disappear.

- Only different distributions of outcomes.
- Different alignments of values.
- Different ways of participating.

So the question becomes:

> **Where do you stand?**

- What are you willing to carry?
- What are you no longer willing to ignore?
- What do <span style="text-decoration: underline;">you</span> optimize for — on purpose

➖

**Standing somewhere — _on purpose_ — restores agency.**

Because staying aligned with the system is a choice.

... following the way is a choice.

... adapting around it is a choice.

- You may choose speed over precision.
- You may choose alignment over friction.
- You may choose what others have already accepted.
- You may choose practicality over compliance

The point is, you have a choice. And how you choose to exercise it is also a choice.

➖

But once you see it — you’re no longer just in it.

You’re agreeing to it.

Not because it removes the system.

But because it changes your relationship to it.

---

## Standing Happens at Every Level

Standing somewhere isn’t just personal.

It happens at every layer of the system.

➡️ **_At the Individual (IC) Level_**

You choose how you participate.

- What you prioritize
- What you ignore
- What you optimize for in your own work

Even inside constraints… you still make choices. How you show up is one of them.

➡️ **_At a Team Leadership Level_**

You translate the system.

You don’t just receive incentives and constraints — you interpret them.

- What gets emphasized to the team
- What gets deprioritized to the team
- What gets protected

You can enforce standards… or adapt to friction.

You can inherit misalignment… or amplify it.

Where you choose to stand orients both you and the team.

➡️ **_At the Leadership Level_**

You define what the system rewards.

- What gets measured
- What gets funded
- What gets celebrated

And just as importantly — what doesn’t.

What you say matters.

**But what you reinforce matters more.**

Because people don’t follow words. 

**==They follow what gets rewarded, protected, and allowed.==**

> Every metric is a stance.<br>Every system is a set of tradeoffs — chosen, whether acknowledged or not.

The system isn’t just something you’re inside of.

It’s something you’re helping create — whether you mean to or not.

---

## What This Looks Like

It doesn’t require grand gestures.

Sometimes it’s small.

-> Choosing depth over speed<br>-> Choosing fewer, more intentional decisions<br>-> Choosing presence over output<br>-> Choosing understanding over appearance

Not perfectly.

But deliberately.

➖

The systems won’t stop.

- They will continue to shape behavior.
- To distribute tradeoffs.
- To reward certain paths over others.

That doesn’t change.

What changes… is whether you see it.

And once you do — whether you drift with it… or stand somewhere.

On purpose.

---

## The orientation to hang with

> **you don’t escape systems<br><br>you choose how you stand within them**

Because how you show up doesn’t stay contained.

- It shapes what gets reinforced.
- It signals what’s acceptable.
- It becomes part of the way.

And whether you realize it or not — others orient around it.

Including you.
