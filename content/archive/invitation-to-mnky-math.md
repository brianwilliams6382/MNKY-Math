# Why This Needed a Name

Most systems don’t announce themselves.

They don’t tell you when they’re nudging a choice, shaping a habit, or quietly narrowing the range of acceptable behavior. They simply sit there. Counting, measuring, rewarding, and reacting; until their influence feels natural.

Normal.

Invisible.

MNKY Math exists because once you notice that influence, it becomes impossible to unsee.

And pretending those systems are neutral starts to feel dishonest.


## Why “Math”

Math is the language systems use to justify themselves.

Targets. Scores. Thresholds. Forecasts. Models. Metrics.

Math gives structure to decisions and credibility to outcomes. It makes tradeoffs feel objective. It gives distance between intent and impact.

But math doesn’t just describe behavior… It shapes it.

- What gets measured gets noticed.
- What gets noticed gets optimized.
- What gets optimized quietly becomes the point. 

MNKY Math isn’t about equations. It’s about what happens around them, and because of them.

It’s about the moment when a number stops being an indicator and starts becoming a steering mechanism.


## Why “MNKY”

Because none of us operate outside these systems.

A monkey is someone under influence. And that applies to each and every person: Frontline workers. Managers. Executives. Designers. Analysts. Leaders. Doctors. Politicians. 

> Everyone.

Because we all exist and move through structures we didn’t fully design. In fact, we’re often co-habiting multiple systems at the same time: the apps on our phones, the messages that reach us across countless channels, the roles we play at work… and even at home.

In that sense, we’re all monkey — because some system is always working to influence outcomes, and we adapt to those systems, often without realizing it.

MNKY is about bringing recognition to that influence.

### _Being MNKY doesn’t mean being powerless._

It means being human inside a system.

And here’s the part that matters most… MNKY should feel like you.

- Because you show up.
- Because you operate with intent.
- Because you do your best within constraints that are rarely neutral.

MNKY isn’t an insult. MNKY isn’t about who you are. 

> MNKY is an acknowledgment about the forces acting on you.


## What MNKY Math Is (and Isn’t)

MNKY Math isn’t here to attack metrics.

It’s here to talk openly about how they’re used.

We’re not anti-data. We’re not anti-measurement. We’re not anti-performance.

We are…

- skeptical of systems that confuse precision with truth.
- wary of numbers that start manufacturing the behavior they were meant to observe.
- interested in what gets lost when optimization becomes automatic.

MNKY Math lives in the space between:

- Indicator and optics
- Measurement and meaning
- Outcome and intent

Not to dismantle systems — but to make them and all of their impacts visible again. Because math is also the language people use to justify what exists.

Systems don’t just reflect behavior.

And once you notice the shaping, the question changes.

It’s no longer: How do we improve the metric?

It becomes:

- Why does this system exist in the first place?
- Who does it serve?
- What else does it move, as it does its job?
- What behavior does it reward?

## A Place to Stand

MNKY Math exists to give language to that noticing; especially when effects extend beyond what anyone intended.

Not answers. Not prescriptions. Not commandments.

Just orientation. So you can hold your own answers, rules, and priorities up to the light.

Because awareness changes posture. Posture changes behavior.

> And behavior — over time — reshapes systems.

You don’t stop being MNKY by opting out.

You become a different kind of MNKY by noticing where you are, your role in the system, how you’re being shaped, and how your role contributes to shaping others — and choosing how you show up anyway.

That’s the math worth hanging with.
