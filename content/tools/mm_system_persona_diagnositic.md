# System Persona Diagnostic

- What does this system want me to notice?
- What does it want me to do next?
- What does it reward?
- What does it make easier?
- What does it make harder?
- What does it measure?
- What does it ignore?
- What kind of person does this system benefit from me becoming?
- What kind of participation does this system make feel normal?
- Where do I still have choice?
