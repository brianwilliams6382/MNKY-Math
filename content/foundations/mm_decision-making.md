6/5/26

This Foundations content emerged as we were assembling the decision-making definition. In challenging the first draft I offered this prompt:

I like this definition. But I feel we need to incorporate personal bias somehow because it is the combination of bias and conditions. This is why we see different people demonstrate different behaviors within the same system. For example, a person how has issues with authority is going to respond differently than a person who tends to be more compliant. So we need room for human biology and phycological influence. I believe this is one of the things that separates MNKY Math because we try to consider these different personas. This also feels like this addition begins shaping a Foundations piece on decision-making.

==Chat offered this as a starting point:==

**Decision-Making Is Not Just Choosing**

Core thesis:

> Decisions are not isolated acts of will. They are situated responses shaped by system conditions and human interpretation.

That would give you room to explore the human/persona side more fully without overloading the definition page.
