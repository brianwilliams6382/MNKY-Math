### Adjacent neighbor

An **Adjacent neighbor** shares useful territory with MNKY Math, but is not central to the framework.  
  
Adjacent neighbors may touch similar concerns, such as behavior, systems, decision-making, incentives, design, work, culture, technology, or measurement.  
  
They are useful because they add context, comparison, vocabulary, or supporting insight.  
  
However, an Adjacent neighbor does not sit at the center of MNKY Math’s core work.  
  
It may help explain one part of the framework without strongly shaping the framework as a whole.
