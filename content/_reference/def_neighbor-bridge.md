## Bridge neighbor

A **Bridge neighbor** helps one enter MNKY Math through a more familiar idea, practice, discipline, or framework.  
  
Bridge neighbors are useful because they begin as a known starting point.  
  
You may not yet understand MNKY Math, but you may already understand Agile, OKRs, behavioral economics, systems thinking, Goodhart’s Law, customer experience, product management, or organizational design.  
  
A Bridge neighbor creates a path from something familiar into something MNKY Math is trying to make more visible.  
  
The bridge matters because MNKY Math often names patterns people have already experienced but may not yet have language for.
