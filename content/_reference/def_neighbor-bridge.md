### Bridge neighbor

A **Bridge neighbor** helps readers enter MNKY Math through a more familiar idea, practice, discipline, or framework.  
  
Bridge neighbors are useful because they give people a known starting point.  
  
A reader may not yet understand MNKY Math, but they may already understand Agile, OKRs, behavioral economics, systems thinking, Goodhart’s Law, customer experience, product management, or organizational design.  
  
A Bridge neighbor creates a path from something familiar into something MNKY Math is trying to make more visible.  
  
The bridge matters because MNKY Math often names patterns people have already experienced but may not yet have language for.
