# The First Walk Doesn't End Here

You've taken a first walk through MNKY Math.

Along the way, you've encountered systems that shape behavior, measurements that influence what happens next, and the idea that we're not simply observers of the systems around us.

We're participants too.

This first walk was never intended to show you everything. 

It was meant to give you a few things to notice.

And perhaps a few new questions to carry with you.

## Where to go from here

There isn't one right direction.

You can explore the garden, following the ideas and connections that interest you.

You can learn more about what drives MNKY Math and how the framework continues to evolve.

You can follow the publishing sequence, discovering new observations and perspectives as they're shared.

Or you can join the conversation, bringing your own questions, experiences, and challenges into the equation.

> You came here invited to **Look like a MNKY**.

What that means to you may be different now.

Or maybe you're still figuring it out.

Either way, the garden remains open.

**The path was here to help you begin.**

Where you go next is yours to decide.
