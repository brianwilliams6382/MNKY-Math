## Automaticity  
  
**Automaticity** is the ability to respond or act with little conscious attention because a behavior has become familiar, repeated, rewarded, or system-trained.

Repetition matters. As an action or response is repeated, it can require less active thought and begin to feel almost automatic.

In sports and music, this is often called “muscle memory.”

In systems, it can become the muscle memory of participation, interpretation, compliance, avoidance, or response.
  
In MNKY Math, automaticity matters because systems often depend on it.  
  
Systems train people to notice certain signals, follow certain paths, avoid certain friction, protect certain [[mm_bananas|bananas]], and repeat certain behaviors because those behaviors become easier, safer, faster, or more rewarding over time.  
  
Automaticity is not failure.  
  
It is part of being human inside systems.  
  
But automaticity becomes important to notice when the system is teaching people to repeat behavior that no longer serves the intended outcome.  
  
A monkey often acts through automaticity.  
  
MNKY begins when awareness enters the loop.

## Two expressions of automaticity

Automaticity can appear in at least two ways.

First, it can appear as trained participation inside a system. People learn where to click, what to ignore, when to comply, when to stay quiet, what to repeat, and how to keep the system moving.

> This is the muscle memory of participation.

Second, automaticity can appear as **trained response to a system**.

People may react quickly with blame, praise, certainty, defense, avoidance, or a familiar explanation before they have looked closely at the conditions shaping what happened.

> This is the muscle memory of interpretation.

Both forms matter.

A system can train people to participate automatically.

It can also train people, leaders, customers, observers, or outsiders to explain what happened automatically.

Automaticity often depends on [[def_patterned-susceptibility|patterned susceptibility]]. Systems can train repeated response because human beings are different, but not infinitely different.

Enough people respond to recurring signals, rewards, pressures, defaults, constraints, and friction in patterned ways for systems to make certain responses more likely.

When automaticity is engaged, the first response may feel reasonable, obvious, or complete before inquiry has begun.

Sometimes inquiry never enters the picture.

### Related interpretation patterns

Automaticity may show up through different interpretation response patterns.

- An [[def_automatic-response|automatic response]] is a fast reaction that feels obvious before it has been examined.<br><br>

- An [[def_avoidance-response|avoidance response]] is a specific form of response that moves away from information, action, responsibility, discomfort, or threat.

Automaticity describes the trained ease or speed of the response.

The response terms describe how that automaticity expresses itself.
