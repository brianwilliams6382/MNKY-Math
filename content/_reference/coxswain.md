### Coxswain

A **coxswain** (pronounced "cox-in") is the person on a rowing crew (the rowing shell) who helps steer the boat, call rhythm, read conditions, and coordinate the crew. The coxswain does not provide the rowing power, but helps direct how that power is applied.

https://usrowing.org/learn-about-rowing/overview/rowing-101
