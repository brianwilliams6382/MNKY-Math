## Ritual

**A ritual is a repeated and structured sequence of actions that carries meaning beyond simply completing the practical task.**

The additional meaning may be:

- personal
- social
- cultural
- symbolic
- spiritual
- emotional
- identity-related
- preparatory

Research definitions commonly distinguish rituals through structured, repeated action combined with symbolism or meaning; ritual can serve emotional regulation, performance, and social-connection functions.

A morning coffee can be routine.

A morning coffee performed in a particular way because it marks the beginning of _your_ day may become ritual.

An athlete may follow a pre-performance ritual not merely because those actions complete a practical task, but because the sequence itself has acquired significance.

### Ritual is not the same thing as routine

A [routine](def_routine) primarily describes recurring structure.

A ritual adds **meaning** to structure.

A routine can therefore become ritualized without ceasing to be a routine.

Rituals also tend to place greater importance on _how_ the sequence is performed than purely instrumental routines do.

### Ritual is not compulsion

A ritual can be freely chosen, enjoyable, meaningful, and easily interrupted.

A [compulsion](def_compulsion) involves a felt pressure or necessity to perform behavior despite conflict with broader goals.

A ritual can become compulsive.

But:

> **Meaningful repetition is not evidence of compulsion.**

Likewise, ritual may contain [habits](def_habit) and [automaticity](def_automaticity), particularly when the sequence has been performed many times.

Ritual describes what the repeated sequence **means**.

Habit and automaticity describe aspects of **how readily it occurs**.

Compulsion describes **how difficult it feels not to perform**.


---
### Related concepts

These concepts can overlap, but they describe different properties of repeated behavior rather than stages of a progression.

- [Routine](def_routine) describes **structure**.
- [Habit](def_habit) describes **learned cue-response tendency**.
- [Automaticity](def_automaticity) describes **how readily something occurs**.
- [Ritual](def_ritual) describes **meaning**.
- [Mastery](def_mastery) describes **developed capability**.
- [Compulsion](def_compulsion) describes **reduced behavioral flexibility**.
- [Addiction](def_addiction) describes **a broader clinical condition**.
