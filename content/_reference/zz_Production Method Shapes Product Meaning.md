## Production Method Shapes Product Meaning

Possible seed definition:

> **Production Method Shapes Product Meaning** is the idea that the way an outcome is produced also produces behavioral, relational, and cultural effects that become part of the outcome itself.

Or shorter:

> **How we produce something becomes part of what we produce.**

That is likely the seed.

This also gives you a clean contrast:

> Most systems ask: Did we get the outcome?  
> MNKY Math asks: What did the system become while producing it?
