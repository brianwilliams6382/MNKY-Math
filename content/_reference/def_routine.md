## Routine

**A routine is a recurring sequence or pattern of actions organized around familiar conditions, timing, place, or purpose.**

A routine describes **structure**.

It may be deliberately created:

- the sequence used to open a store
- a morning exercise routine
- preparing for bed in a particular order

Or it may emerge quietly through repetition:

- parking in the same general area
- showering in roughly the same sequence
- listening to the same radio program during the same commute
- putting the same leg into your pants first

Research on routine sequential action similarly treats familiar everyday activities as organized sequences that can increasingly rely on learned structure while greater top-down control becomes important when something novel interrupts them.

### Routine is not the same thing as habit

A **routine describes the recurring structure**.

A [habit](def_habit) describes a learned response that becomes increasingly tied to recurring cues or conditions and can be initiated with less conscious deliberation.

A routine can contain habits.

But it does not have to.

You can deliberately perform a routine while remaining consciously aware of each step.

### How routine relates to the others

Repetition can produce a routine.

Repeatedly performing parts of a routine under similar conditions can contribute to habit formation, but routine does not inevitably become habit. Habit research supports this importance of repeated behavior in stable contexts.

A routine can also become a [ritual](def_ritual) when the sequence carries meaning beyond simply completing the task.

A routine can contain compulsive or addictive behavior, but routine itself does not imply compulsion, impaired control, or harm.

> **A routine tells us something about the structure of repetition.  
> It does not, by itself, tell us why the person keeps repeating it.**

### Related concepts

These concepts can overlap, but they describe different properties of repeated behavior rather than stages of a progression.

- [Routine](def_routine) describes **structure**.
- [Habit](def_habit) describes **learned cue-response tendency**.
- [Automaticity](def_automaticity) describes **how readily something occurs**.
- [Ritual](def_ritual) describes **meaning**.
- [Mastery](def_mastery) describes **developed capability**.
- [Compulsion](def_compulsion) describes **reduced behavioral flexibility**.
- [Addiction](def_addiction) describes **a broader clinical condition**.
