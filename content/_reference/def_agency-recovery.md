## Agency Recovery

**Agency recovery** is the process of noticing where a system has shaped choice, attention, participation, or response, and then reclaiming some capacity to act with greater awareness and intention.

Agency recovery does not mean becoming free from all influence.

**==It means recovering the ability to see influence more clearly, recognize more moments as choices, and participate with less automaticity.==**

Agency recovery is closely related to [[def_agency|agency]], but it begins from the recognition that agency may have already been narrowed, redirected, trained, or made less visible by the system.
