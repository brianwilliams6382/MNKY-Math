## Opinion

An **opinion** is a position, judgment, interpretation, or preference a person holds about something.  
  
Opinions are often treated as personal truth.  
  
MNKY Math treats opinion as something shaped. 
  
An opinion is an outcome. It emerges from experience, identity, memory, family, culture, fear, loyalty, information, incentives, social pressure, emotional history, repeated exposure, the systems a person has lived inside, and the need to feel coherent.
  
This does not make the opinion fake.  
  
It makes the opinion traceable.  

Take a moment and consider this: _"If there are countless possible opinions someone could hold, then the one I currently hold is not inevitable. It is shaped. It has a history. It came from somewhere."_
  
The question is not only:  What is my opinion?  
  
The deeper question is:  Why this opinion, from all the possible opinions I could have held?
  
- Why this opinion?  
- What influenced it?  
- What did this opinion help me protect, express, avoid, preserve, or belong to?  
- What might I believe if different experiences, signals, relationships, fears, or systems had shaped me?  
  
Opinions matter because people often use them to push outward before examining where they came from inwardly.  
  
A MNKY does not treat opinion as worthless.  
  
A MNKY treats opinion as evidence.
