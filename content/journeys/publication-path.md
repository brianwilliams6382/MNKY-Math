# The Publication Path

MNKY Math did not begin as a finished system.

It began as a series of observations, questions, tensions, and patterns. One idea led to another. Over time, the ideas began to form a larger way of seeing.

The **Publication Path** lets you read MNKY Math in the order its thinking becomes visible, one essay at a time.

This Journey is designed for readers who want to start at the beginning and move forward one step at a time.

## Why this path exists

MNKY Math is a digital garden. You can enter through many doors.

But if you are new here, too many doors can become its own kind of [[def_friction|friction]].

The Publication Path gives you a structured way to begin. It follows published MNKY Math essays in sequence, tracing the order in which the thinking becomes visible, one essay at a time.

Each page is a stepping stone. Some stepping stones introduce core ideas. Some examine everyday examples. Some expose system patterns. Some begin naming the tensions that MNKY Math continues to explore.

This path is especially useful if you want to understand not only what MNKY Math says, but how its ideas appear in the [[def_system|systems]] we move through every day.

Taken together, the path shows the gradual movement from simple observations toward a larger question:

**How do systems shape behavior, and how do those behaviors shape outcomes?**

## How to use this Journey

Use the Journey card below to move from one stop to the next.

From there, use the Journey navigation to move forward or backward through the sequence.

You do not need to read everything in one sitting. The path is meant to be returned to.

If a page contains a link that catches your attention, you can preview that linked page without leaving the current stop. This lets you look sideways without losing the path forward.

Opening a linked page will take you into the wider garden. That is part of the design. You can use your browser’s back button to return to the Journey, or come back to this page and begin again.

## What to notice as you move

As you walk this path, pay attention to the ideas that repeat.

Look for systems.

Look for incentives.

Look for metrics.

Look for tradeoffs.

Look for moments where something that appears personal, individual, or isolated begins to look more structural.

Ask what the system is producing.

Ask what behavior the system is teaching.

Ask what is being optimized, and what is being ignored.

That shift is central to MNKY Math.

The goal is not to memorize every article. The goal is to begin seeing the relationships between systems, behavior, and outcomes with more clarity.

MNKY Math can be read in different ways. You may move through a page as an observer, a participant, a builder, or someone beginning to model the system underneath the surface.

These [[mm_reader-modes|Reader Modes]] are not rules. They are ways of noticing what changes when you change how you read.
