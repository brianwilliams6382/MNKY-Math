MNKY Math is built as a digital garden. It is meant to be explored through links, definitions, neighbors, articles, ideas, and paths that branch.

But sometimes a visitor does not want to wander.

Sometimes they want a path.

Journeys are curated reading paths through MNKY Math. Each Journey sequences selected pages into a deliberate order, so a reader can move from one stepping stone to the next without needing to return to the Explorer, search the Archive, or decide where to go after every page.

A Journey does not replace the garden.

It gives the garden a path.

## Why Journeys exist

Many MNKY Math pages are meant to stand on their own. Others become more meaningful when they are read in sequence.

A Journey helps with that sequence.

Journeys may be used to:

- read published essays in order
- follow a developing theme
- learn a MNKY Math foundation or model
- move through a related set of definitions
- explore a system pattern from multiple angles
- eventually apply MNKY Math diagnostics in a more guided way

Each Journey is a way of saying:

Start here.<br>Then go here.<br>Then keep going.


## How Journeys work

Each Journey contains a sequenced set of pages. These pages are the stepping stones of the path.

A Journey usually begins on a summary page. This page explains what the Journey is for, what kind of path it follows, and how to begin.

Some Journey pages may also include a list of the stops in the path, along with short notes explaining why each stop belongs in the sequence.

Once you are on a Journey, a Journey card will appear near the bottom of the page. The card shows the current Journey, your position in the sequence, and the previous or next stop when one is available.

At the beginning of a Journey, the card will point you toward the first stop.

As you move through the Journey, the card lets you continue forward, step back, or understand where you are in the path.

At the end of a Journey, the card will show that you have reached the end of the path.

Journeys are meant to give structure without closing the garden. You can follow the path directly, pause at any stop, or use previews and links to explore nearby ideas along the way.

The path is there to guide you, not to trap you.


## Leaving a Journey

MNKY Math pages often include links to related ideas, definitions, neighbors, and supporting concepts.

Following one of those links is encouraged.

But opening that link means you are stepping off the current Journey path and back into the wider garden.

If you want to explore a linked idea without leaving the Journey, use the preview feature. Hovering over a link will let you read or sample the linked page while staying on the current stepping stone.

This allows you to look sideways without losing the path forward.

If you do open a linked page, you can usually return to the Journey using your browser’s back button, or you can return to the Journeys directory and begin again from the Journey page.

## Current Journeys

Journeys will expand over time as MNKY Math grows.

Some Journeys will be broad. Others will be narrow. Some will follow publication order. Others will follow a concept, a model, a tension, or a diagnostic question.

The first major Journey is the [[publication-path|Publication Path]]: a sequenced way to read MNKY Math essays in the order they are published.

This path is especially useful for new visitors who want to understand how the thinking becomes visible.

## A path, not a tunnel

Journeys are structured, but they are not meant to trap you.

The garden remains open.

The links remain alive.

The path is there when you want it.

The wider system is there when you are ready to wander.
