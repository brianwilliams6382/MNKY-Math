# Participation Standards  
  
MNKY Math is an evolving public body of work. At this stage, the repository is primarily maintained by its author and is not operating as an open contribution project.  
  
Feedback, questions, and thoughtful challenges are welcome through the contact paths listed on mnkymath.com.  
  
Participation around MNKY Math should be curious, respectful, specific, and oriented toward clearer understanding.  
  
Disagreement is welcome. Dismissiveness, harassment, bad-faith argument, spam, or extractive use of the work is not.
