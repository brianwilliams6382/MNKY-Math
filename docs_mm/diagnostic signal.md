## “Diagnostic Signal” may be better than “Prescription”

I would be careful with Gemini’s suggested “Prescription” language.

Your instinct to avoid “do X and get Y” is right. The consulting potential of MNKY Math should not come from having universal prescriptions. It should come from diagnosing the actual system before recommending interventions.

So instead of:

> Prescription: Re-establish a floor.

I’d consider:

> Possible intervention: Re-establish a floor.  
> Diagnostic caution: This only helps if the hidden issue is ambiguity, not workload, trust, or staffing.

That keeps you out of methodology theater.

A stronger recurring structure might be:

**Diagnostic Signal**

- **Visible metric:** What improved or appears healthy?
- **Hidden tradeoff:** What moved elsewhere?
- **Behavioral evidence:** What are people doing differently?
- **Likely system pattern:** What recurring pattern does this suggest?
- **Intervention question:** What would need to be true before acting?

That is much more aligned with your philosophy.
