## 1. The deeper site shift is from “article feed” to “diagnostic library”

The strongest new idea is that MNKY Math should not become merely a repository of polished essays. It should increasingly behave like a **reference system**: articles, lenses, variables, patterns, diagnostics, and eventually tools.

That matters because your own stated direction is not simply “writing about systems.” You are trying to move toward structured frameworks that help organizations diagnose where metrics, methods, incentives, and behaviors are failing to produce the intended outcome. The exchange names this well as a move from “Methodology Theatre” to “Systemic Efficacy” and frames MNKY Math less as another business-method brand and more as a diagnostic lens for seeing where the adopted method is no longer serving the system.

That has implications for the website:

- Articles should remain readable as standalone essays.
- But each article can also become a node in a larger diagnostic map.
- The site should eventually let a reader move by **problem type**, not just by publication date.
- Tags should not be casual labels; they should become part of the framework.

So instead of only:

> L21 — The Tradeoffs You Refuse to See

The website version might also carry:

> Primary pattern: Responsibility Redistribution  
> Active variables: Incentive Design, Measurement Ghosts, Psychological Safety, Boundary Drift  
> Diagnostic signal: The metric improves while the human hesitation increases.

That is where MNKY Math starts becoming more than writing.
