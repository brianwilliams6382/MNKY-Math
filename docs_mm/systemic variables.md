## 5. “Systemic Variables” may be the missing bridge between essay and framework

The proposed “Systemic Variables” section is one of the most actionable website ideas.

This does not have to appear under every article immediately, but for site-native versions, we could experiment with a small bottom section such as:

**Systemic Variables**

- **Human signal:** hesitation, avoidance, compliance, repeated engagement
- **System response:** reward, friction, escalation, recommendation, pricing, surveillance
- **Metric focus:** what the system appears to be optimizing
- **Ghost outcome:** what changes but may not be measured
- **Agency point:** where awareness can alter participation

This could become the MNKY Math equivalent of an article footer, but more useful than a generic “key takeaways” section.
