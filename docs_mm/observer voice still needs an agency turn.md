## 3. The observer voice still needs an agency turn

The exchange surfaces a real risk: observation can become disempowering if the article only shows readers how deeply systems shape them. The line that matters most is the pivot from:

> “This is how you are controlled”

to:

> “This is the signal you are sending.”

That is a major MNKY Math distinction. The writing can remain non-prescriptive while still restoring agency.

A useful pattern for future articles might be:

1. **Reveal the system** — what is shaping behavior?
2. **Name the signal** — what does the person’s behavior tell the system?
3. **Expose the feedback loop** — how does the system respond?
4. **Restore agency** — what becomes visible once the participant understands the loop?

This avoids “do these five things” advice while still giving the reader something to hold.
