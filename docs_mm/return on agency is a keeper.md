## 2. “Return on Agency” is a keeper

Gemini’s phrase — **Return on Agency** — is extremely strong.

It clarifies the “Math” in MNKY Math without reducing it to literal equations. You are not just critiquing the way organizations use math to optimize revenue, engagement, productivity, or compliance. You are asking what happens when humans understand the math well enough to participate with more awareness.

That gives you a clean contrast:

> Most systems use math to optimize return on investment.  
> MNKY Math asks what happens when people use systemic literacy to recover return on agency.

That idea could become part of the site’s About language, or possibly a recurring internal standard for article prep. Every article does not need to say “agency,” but each article should probably answer:

> What agency does this lens restore?

That is a powerful editorial test.
